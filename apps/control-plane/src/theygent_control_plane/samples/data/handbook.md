# TheYgent — Company Handbook (fictional demo)

This is the demo knowledge base behind the Handbook Q&A sample. It treats TheYgent as a
fictional company so the sample has something real to search — **every fact in it is
invented** and carries no meaning outside the sample.

## About TheYgent

In this demo universe, TheYgent is a small software company founded in 2019, with one
office and a fully remote engineering team. Its fictional customers and their orders also
seed the private-SQL sample's demo database, so the two samples share one consistent world.

## Data retention

Customer account data is kept for **3 years after the last completed order**, then deleted
in the quarterly purge. Operational telemetry (service logs, usage metrics) is kept for
**90 days**. Invoices are kept for **10 years** to satisfy commercial record-keeping rules.
Exceptions to any retention period require written approval from the **Data Protection
Officer (DPO)** — team leads cannot approve exceptions.

## Expense policy

Expenses under **200 EUR** are self-approved with a receipt. Expenses from **200 to 1000
EUR** need team-lead approval. Anything above **1000 EUR** needs the CFO's approval and a
purchase order raised in advance. Alcohol and per-seat software subscriptions are never
reimbursable.

## Support SLAs

Standard support tickets get a first response within **24 hours** on business days;
critical outages within **1 hour**, around the clock. A missed critical-outage SLA credits
10% of that month's subscription fee automatically; credits above 500 EUR are reviewed by
the CFO.

## Security contacts

Suspected data incidents go to **security@theygent.example** and must be reported within
**4 hours** of discovery. The on-call rotation is owned by the platform team; the DPO is
looped in for anything involving personal data. Lost hardware is reported to IT first, not
to a team lead.
