How to Deploy TheYgent to Kubernetes

6:41 · Published August 16, 2026 · Watch on YouTube · All videos on YouTube

What This Video Covers

Deploying TheYgent to Kubernetes — Minikube on a laptop in the video, any cluster in practice. Clone the repository and review the manifests under deploy/k8s; resources, volume sizes, and replicas are yours to change.

One make target builds the images, loads them into the cluster, applies the manifests, and waits for the rollout: the interface, control plane, inference plane, and durable worker deployments, plus Postgres as a StatefulSet. After port-forwarding, the first visit creates the admin account, and the planes report healthy. make k8s-down removes everything again.
